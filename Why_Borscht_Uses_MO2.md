# 🥫 Why Borscht Uses Mod Organizer 2

Borscht is built around **Mod Organizer 2 (MO2)** because it provides the cleanest, most reliable way to manage a large mod setup. ☢️

STALKER 2 modding is still evolving. As mod lists grow, managing dozens or hundreds of mods manually becomes increasingly difficult. Borscht uses **MO2 + Wabbajack** to provide a consistent, reproducible installation that is easier to maintain, troubleshoot, and support. 🛠️

---

# ❓ "Why not just install mods manually?"

Manual modding works perfectly fine for a small number of mods. 👍

The problem appears when your mod list grows.

With manual installation:

❌ Files are copied directly into your game folders  
❌ Removing a mod may leave behind files you forgot about  
❌ Troubleshooting requires remembering every change you made  
❌ Updating a large mod list becomes time-consuming  
❌ Two users can end up with completely different installations  

MO2 solves this by keeping mods separated and organized while still allowing the game to see them normally. ✅

---

# 🎮 "Doesn't Steam Workshop already handle mods?"

Steam Workshop is convenient, but it has limitations. ⚠️

Not every mod is available through Workshop. Many of the most popular and ambitious mods are distributed through other platforms, especially Nexus Mods.

Workshop also does not provide the same level of:

✅ Mod ordering control  
✅ Conflict visibility  
✅ Detailed organization  
✅ Advanced troubleshooting tools  
✅ Profile management  

Borscht is designed around having complete control over the entire mod setup. 🎯

---

# 🌀 "Why not Vortex?"

Vortex is a capable mod manager and works well for many users. 👍

However, Borscht is built specifically around MO2 because of how it handles **large, complex mod lists**.

The biggest difference is MO2's **Virtual File System (VFS)**.

Instead of copying files directly into your game installation, MO2 creates a controlled virtual environment.

This means:

✅ Your original game files stay clean  
✅ Mods remain separated from each other  
✅ Removing a mod does not leave behind hidden leftovers  
✅ Multiple profiles can share the same installation  
✅ Troubleshooting is easier because changes are controlled and visible  

One of MO2's greatest advantages is knowing exactly what is active and where it comes from. 🔎
